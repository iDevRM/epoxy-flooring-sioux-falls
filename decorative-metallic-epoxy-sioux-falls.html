Decorative & Metallic Epoxy in Sioux Falls, SD

Decorative and metallic epoxy flooring turns a garage, basement, or showroom floor into a visible feature instead of a plain background surface. We install both color-flake systems and hand-applied metallic pigment finishes for Sioux Falls homes and small businesses.

What Does Decorative & Metallic Epoxy Involve?

Prep work matches a standard floor — diamond grinding, crack repair, and a moisture-appropriate primer. The difference is in the base coat: for color-flake, we broadcast vinyl chips into the wet epoxy for texture and pattern; for metallic, we hand-work mica pigments into the base coat with brushes, squeegees, and sometimes a heat gun to build movement and depth before sealing it under a clear topcoat.

When Do You Need Decorative & Metallic Epoxy?

This fits showroom-style garages, finished basements used as living space, home bars, or any small business floor where appearance matters as much as durability. It is also a popular touch for sellers wanting their Sioux Falls garage to stand out during a home sale.

Why Choose a Decorative Finish Over Solid Color?

A solid color floor is functional but flat. Color flake hides minor imperfections and everyday debris better, which matters in a working garage. Metallic finishes create genuine visual depth — no two metallic floors look identical, since the pigment movement happens by hand during application, which is part of the appeal for homeowners who want a one-of-a-kind result.

What Affects the Cost of Decorative Epoxy in Sioux Falls?

Color-flake systems typically add a modest premium over a solid-color floor, mostly in material cost. Metallic finishes cost more, since they require more skilled hand-application time per square foot and additional material layers. Total project cost still starts from the same $4 to $10 per square foot base range, with decorative work landing toward the upper end.

Color Flake vs. Metallic: Which Do You Need?

Color flake is the better choice for a durable, low-maintenance garage or shop floor that still looks upgraded from solid color — it is more forgiving of dirt and tire marks day to day. Metallic is the better choice when the floor is a design feature in its own right, like a finished basement, home bar, or showroom garage, where the marbled look is the point.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is metallic epoxy different from a regular color epoxy floor?

Metallic epoxy uses mica-based pigments manipulated while wet with tools and sometimes a heat gun, creating a three-dimensional, marbled or pooled effect. A standard solid-color or flake floor looks flat and consistent by comparison.

Does a decorative finish cost more than a standard epoxy floor?

Yes, color-flake adds a modest amount over a solid color floor, and full metallic systems cost more than that since they take more material and skilled hand-application time. We quote each finish separately so you can compare the actual price difference.

Will a decorative epoxy floor hold up as well as a plain one?

Yes, durability comes from the same primer, base coat, and topcoat system underneath the decorative layer, so a color-flake or metallic floor holds up to the same daily use and Sioux Falls winters as a solid-color floor.
